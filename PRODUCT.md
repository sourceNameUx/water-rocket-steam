# Water rocket project

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user's instruction to complete the project without questions. Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, published through GitHub Pages.

## Users

Santiago and the audience reviewing his STEAM school project. A teacher reviewing the submission is an inferred primary visitor.

## Product Purpose

Present the real rocket, its construction, documentary, test results and downloadable report in one accessible place.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site must show real photographs and the corrected final-launch recording. Reported heights are approximately 2 m, 0.5 m and 1 m; the goal is at least 5 m. Material quantities and mass use explicitly stated assumptions. New text is English under the latest session protocol; the retained earlier documentary is Spanish. No login or backend is needed.

## Brand Commitments

Continue the documentary's lively blue, yellow and green identity, with large readable headings and real project material. The separate school report uses a plain black Arial layout.

## Evidence on Hand

`revision-v6/public/design/` and `revision-v6/public/media/` contain project photography and prepared clips. `revision-v6/narration/revision-plan.json` records the final release, quantities and results. `Rocket-Results-and-Analysis-v6.docx` and `.pdf` contain the revised analysis.

## Product Principles

- Let the actual experiment lead.
- Keep estimates visibly distinct from measurements.
- Give the documentary and report direct, working access.
- Make the page readable on phones and classroom computers.
